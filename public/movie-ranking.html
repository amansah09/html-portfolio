The Best Movies According to Aman

My top 5 movies/series of all time

Gullak ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Gullak isn't about grand adventures—it's about family, love, laughter, and the little struggles of middle-class life. Every episode feels like a warm hug, making you laugh one moment and emotional the next. It's simple, relatable, and incredibly comforting.

Spirited Away ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Spirited Away is more than an animated film; it's a masterpiece filled with imagination, emotion, and life lessons. Its breathtaking visuals, unforgettable characters, and beautiful storytelling make it one of the greatest animated movies ever created.

3 Idiots ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

3 Idiots is the perfect mix of comedy, emotion, friendship, and social commentary. It challenges the education system while reminding us to follow our passion. Even after multiple watches, it continues to make you laugh, cry, and think.

No Strings Attached ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

No Strings Attached perfectly balances humor and romance while showing how casual relationships often become more complicated than expected. It's lighthearted, entertaining, and has genuine emotional moments beneath its comedy.

Off Campus ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆

Off Campus captures the excitement and uncertainty of college life with relatable characters and emotional depth. It blends romance, personal growth, and youthful energy into a story that's both engaging and comforting.
